# Calder Car Rentals Homepage Redesign

## 1. Website Overview

Calder Rent A Car LLC (Calder Car Rentals) is a Dubai-based vehicle rental company offering flexible car hire arrangements for residents, tourists, business travellers, and corporate customers.

The redesigned homepage focuses on making rental options easier to discover, improving access to vehicle categories, and creating a clearer customer enquiry journey. The overall direction positions Calder as a convenient car rental provider offering daily, weekly, monthly, and long-term rental arrangements.

## 2. Main Improvements

### 1. Improved Website Accessibility and SEO Presentation
- Prioritised a clearer homepage structure and more accessible presentation of business information.
- Established a more organised content hierarchy to support search visibility and customer navigation.
- Improved the presentation of Calder's car rental services and business identity.
- Addressed the need for a more discoverable and accessible online presence.

### 2. Better Rental Information Discoverability
- Organised rental options into clear categories covering daily, weekly, monthly, and long-term rentals.
- Improved the visibility of available vehicle categories, including economy cars, sedans, and SUVs.
- Created clearer pathways for customers to explore rental options and make enquiries.
- Reduced the effort required to understand the company's rental services.

### 3. Consistent Contact Information and Enquiry Experience
- Prioritised a consistent presentation of primary business contact information.
- Improved the visibility of telephone and email enquiry options.
- Created a clearer route for customers to request rental information.
- Addressed confusion caused by different telephone numbers and email addresses appearing across public business listings.

## 3. Design Improvements

- **Visual presentation:** Improved the hierarchy of rental information, vehicle categories, and customer-facing content.
- **Layout:** Organised rental options and business information into clearer homepage sections.
- **User experience:** Simplified the journey from exploring rental periods to contacting the company.
- **Consistency:** Established a more unified presentation of business identity and contact information.
- **Overall appearance:** Created a more structured and professional homepage focused on rental discovery, customer convenience, and enquiries.

## Project Objective

The redesign addresses the three critical issues identified during the original research: website accessibility and SEO presentation, poor rental information discoverability, and inconsistent contact information. The overall objective is to make Calder's rental services easier to understand, improve the customer journey, and provide clearer ways to enquire about vehicle availability and rental arrangements.
